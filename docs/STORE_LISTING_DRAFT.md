## Aethryon Drive
**Short (≤80):** Browse and transfer files on your own servers — private by design.
**Full:** Aethryon Drive connects to WebDAV and Nextcloud servers (Dropbox and OneDrive are experimental). Everything stays on your device: passwords are kept in the system secure store, folder listings are cached in an encrypted local database for offline browsing and fast search, and nothing is sent to us. Download and upload with progress and cancel, never overwriting your files.
**Honest limits (include in the listing):** early release; Google Drive, Box, pCloud, MEGA not yet supported; no background sync.
## Aethryon Mail
**Short:** A lightweight, private mail reader for IMAP accounts.
**Full:** Read and send mail from iCloud, Yahoo and any IMAP/SMTP account (app password), or sign in to Gmail/Outlook with your own app registration. Messages are shown as safe plain text — no tracking pixels, no scripts. Headers are cached in an encrypted local database; message text is never stored. 
**Honest limits:** inbox only, no attachments yet, no push notifications (checks while the app is open), Proton Mail needs its desktop Bridge and is not supported yet.
