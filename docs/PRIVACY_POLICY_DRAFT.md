# Privacy Policy — DRAFT (Aethryon Drive & Aethryon Mail)
*খসড়া: কোড যা এখন করে তা-ই বর্ণিত। প্রকাশের আগে `[CONTACT-EMAIL]` বসান ও আইনি পর্যালোচনা করান।* Last updated: [DATE]

**Who we are.** Aethryon Labs ("we"). Contact: [CONTACT-EMAIL].

**Short version.** The apps work on your device. We run no servers for them, we collect no analytics, no crash reports, no advertising identifiers, and we cannot see your files, mail or passwords.

**What the apps store on your device**
- Account details you enter (server addresses, user names) and your passwords/sign-in tokens — kept **only in your operating system's secure credential store** (Android Keystore, Windows Credential Manager, Linux Secret Service).
- A local cache, **encrypted** with a key kept in that same secure store: for Drive, the names/sizes/dates of folders you opened; for Mail, message headers (subject, date, flags). Message text and attachments are **not** saved. If encryption is unavailable, nothing is written to disk.
- Files you download are saved in a folder named "Aethryon" in your Downloads (or the app's documents folder on phones).
- Small preferences (for example, the auto-refresh interval).

**Where data goes.** Only to the servers *you* choose (your WebDAV/Nextcloud server, your mail provider, Dropbox, Microsoft, Google), over encrypted connections, to do what you ask. For sign-in with Google, Microsoft or Dropbox you use **your own** app registration (client ID) and sign in in your browser; we never receive your credentials or tokens.

**What we do not do.** No tracking, no selling or sharing of data, no remote content in e-mails (images in messages are never loaded; messages are shown as plain text), no background services.

**Optional features.** AI and sync features, if ever added, will be off by default, will say exactly what data leaves your device, and will need your explicit consent.

**Deleting your data.** Removing an account in the app deletes its password, sign-in tokens and cached data from your device. Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored. Your files and mail on your servers are never touched by these actions.

**Children.** The apps are not directed to children.

**Changes / contact.** We will update this page when behaviour changes. Questions: [CONTACT-EMAIL].
